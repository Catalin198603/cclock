5 minute timer for quick tasks

A five minute countdown is useful for short breaks, quick exercises, classroom activities, tea brewing and focused tasks. The timer runs directly in your browser and shows the remaining time down to milliseconds.

How to use the 5 minute timer

Press Start to begin the five minute countdown. Pause it whenever needed, continue from the same point, or reset it and start again. You can also switch instantly to another preset timer below.
