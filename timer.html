Free countdown timer

Use this timer for studying, cooking, exercise, meetings, breaks and everyday tasks. The countdown runs directly in your browser and shows milliseconds for precise timing.

How to use the timer

Choose a preset or enter hours, minutes and seconds, then press Start. You can pause the countdown, continue it later or reset it at any time.

Timer or alarm clock?

A timer counts down a duration, while an online alarm clock is set for a specific time of day. To measure elapsed time instead, use the online stopwatch.

Sound alarm and fullscreen mode

Enable Sound On to hear an alarm when the countdown reaches zero. Select Fullscreen for a larger display. Keep the page open for reliable alerts.
