A visual timer for teachers and students

Project the countdown on a classroom screen to help students see how much time remains for independent work, group discussions or transitions. The progress bar shrinks as time runs out.

How to use the classroom timer

Choose a preset or enter a custom duration. Press Start, pause when instructions are needed, or reset for the next activity. Use Fullscreen to enlarge the display and Sound Off for silent work.

Classroom activity ideas

Try one minute for transitions, five minutes for a warm-up, ten minutes for partner work or twenty minutes for independent practice. Choose a duration appropriate to your class.

More free time tools
