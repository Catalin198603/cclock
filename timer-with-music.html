Free online timer with music and ambient sounds

Choose a countdown and a background sound for studying, reading, working or relaxing. Adjust the background volume separately from the final alarm.

How to use the music timer

Select a duration and a sound, then turn music on and press Start. Pause the countdown to take a break. Background audio stops when the countdown finishes or is reset. Keep the browser open and device awake for reliable playback.

Choose your focus sound

Try gentle synthesized ambient tones, rain-like noise, ocean waves, forest-like sound textures, white noise or brown noise. All sounds are synthesized locally in your browser.
