How to use the online alarm clock

cClock works directly in your browser, so there is nothing to install. Choose a time for any alarm, switch it on and keep this page open so the alarm can ring at the selected time.

  1. Choose the alarm time you want.
  2. Turn on the switch next to that alarm.
  3. Leave the cClock alarm page open in your browser.

Set multiple alarms online

You can configure up to 8 different alarms for work, study, breaks, reminders or daily routines. Each alarm can be activated or deactivated independently, and your settings are stored locally in your browser on this device.
