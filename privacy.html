Privacy Policy

Draft — not for production until controller details and advertising configuration are confirmed.

Controller

The operator of cClock is responsible for processing personal data. For privacy inquiries, email cclock.eu@gmail.com. The controller's full identity and legally required contact information must be completed before publication.

Hosting

cClock is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting may involve processing IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The separate development preview is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Its hosting provider may process technical request data to deliver and protect the preview. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Google Analytics is disabled on the preview hostname.

Local storage

cClock stores preferences and privacy choices in your browser. These support the functionality you request and remember your settings.

Google Analytics

Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after analytics consent. It helps measure visits and usage. You can change your choice using Privacy settings in the site footer. Analytics consent is separate from advertising consent.

Advertising

cClock plans to use Google AdSense. When enabled, Google and advertising partners may process identifiers, device and browser information, IP addresses and advertising interactions, and use cookies or similar technologies subject to applicable consent requirements. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Legal bases and your choices

Necessary processing may rely on legitimate interests in providing and securing the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Optional analytics and advertising storage requiring consent rely on consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable device-storage rules). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

International transfers and retention

Service providers may process data outside the EEA. Applicable safeguards and actual retention periods must be verified and documented before publication.
